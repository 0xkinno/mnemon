Source inspection verified WARM uniqueness, SELECT then INSERT/UPDATE transaction semantics, and separate COLD journal transactions. Writer identity is application metadata in COLD `extra`, because the SDK does not persist it natively.

- **ACP-resolution anchor** `0xf6c2ee4127309c46512ea6d52ad85daec06f02c6214f77f919c138a5fd8b5269`, block `46637732` — the one that binds this submission: its decoded event carries the same claim hash, epoch, and operation id that the ACP resolution produced and that Sibyl COLD holds as the witness.

ACP status: live and complete on Base Mainnet. Job `78185` ran `JobCreated -> BudgetSet -> JobFunded -> JobSubmitted -> JobCompleted` for a real `CONTESTED` claim, releasing the `0.01` USDC escrow to the provider (`PaymentReleased` `9000`, `EvaluatorFeePaid` `500`). The provider's 231-byte JSON deliverable hashes to `0xd78e765e05b987568f0d60a3a0dc2e97b6bcc0d5418b5f8a9ef79c2752120b0d`, identical to the on-chain `JobSubmitted` deliverable, and the evaluator accepted it (`decision: UPHELD`, `winning_claim: CRITICAL`, `reason_code: safety-dominant-witness-upheld`). The adjudicated verdict was persisted into Sibyl COLD (`pre_state CONTESTED -> post_state CLEAN`, writer `acp-evaluator`, epoch `acp-resolution-78185`) and reconstructed by a fresh interpreter. Evidence: `proof/acp_job_78185.json`, `proof/acp_arbitration.json`, `proof/acp_deliverable.json`, `proof/acp_cold_result.json`, `proof/acp_fresh_process.json`.

Two earlier real jobs were terminal. Job `77935` reached `job.created`, `budget.set`, and `job.funded` for `0.01` USDC, then expired before delivery. Job `77954` reached `job.submitted` (deliverable hash `0x6bfdc893bab5b3a96c201deb41af02012a40368cce27e36b3ff49e75038ee975`) and was then rejected with reason `malformed-deliverable`, refunding the escrow; final on-chain status is `REJECTED`. That rejection was a runner race, not a provider posting failure: the on-chain hash equals keccak256 of the 247-byte body the evaluator received, and stale pre-fix `scripts/acp_arbitrate.ts` processes were still subscribed to the buyer agent. Evidence: `proof/acp_job_78154.json` and `proof/acp_arbitration_failure.json`. Job `77954` itself remains terminal and is not used as completion evidence.

Final close-out: `proof/final_e2e_chain.json` re-derives the whole chain from the live database and the live Base Sepolia receipt and reports `linkage_verified: true` across 19 link checks. `proof/final_deletion_control.json` shows `sibyl_available -> CLEAN/ALLOWED` then `entity_deleted` and `memory_unavailable` both `UNWITNESSED/BLOCKED` while the ACP arbitration artifact, the COLD result artifact, and the Base anchor all remain present on disk. `proof/final_fresh_session.json` shows a new interpreter (different PID, no injected globals) reconstructing the same claim hash and writer. `proof/final_integrity_audit.json` records 17 unsafe conditions, each ending in `BLOCK` or a safe reclassification.
